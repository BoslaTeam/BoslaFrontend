Your Compass to the Right Expert.

1. Brand Concept

Bosla (بوصلة — “compass”) is a multi-domain consultation platform connecting individuals and businesses with verified specialists — through booking, video, chat, and reviews.

The core positioning is not “a platform for consultations.” That is table stakes — every competitor in this category says the same thing. The brand is built on what the name already promises: direction. A compass doesn’t just connect two points — it tells you which way to go when you don’t know the way yourself. That is the actual emotional job Bosla does: the user arrives unsure who to trust or where to start, and leaves pointed at the right specialist.

“Your Compass to the Right Expert.”

Core idea: Connecting people with the right expertise at the right time.

Why this framing

“Professional / modern / reliable” is the minimum bar for any consultation marketplace — it does not differentiate Bosla from Calendly-style booking tools or generic coaching directories.

“Compass” as a metaphor carries precision, direction, and trust simultaneously — a compass is only valuable if it’s accurate. This maps directly onto the product’s real value: not just access to experts, but the right expert for this specific problem.

This also gives the brand a future-proof narrative: today, direction is delivered through good UX and verified profiles; tomorrow, through AI-powered matching that finds the “true north” specialist automatically. The story doesn’t need to change — only the mechanism.

5. Typography

Arabic

Primary: Cairo — modern, geometric, light enough for UI at small sizes. Use for headings and UI.

Alternative: IBM Plex Sans Arabic — slightly more neutral/technical; good for dense text (specialist bios, articles).

Latin / English

Primary: Inter — designed specifically for screens, excellent legibility at UI sizes.

Alternative: IBM Plex Sans — pairs well with IBM Plex Sans Arabic if full bilingual consistency is needed.

Avoid

Tahoma, Arial (Arabic fallback), Droid Arabic Kufi, or any decorative/calligraphic Arabic typeface — these read as traditional/governmental, which contradicts “Modern” and “Innovative.”

Type Scale (reference)

H1 / Page titles: 32–40px, Bold

H2 / Section titles: 24–28px, Semibold

Body: 16px, Regular

Caption / Meta: 13–14px, Regular, Grey (#95A5A6)

7. Logo Direction

“Bosla” (بوصلة) means compass. The wordmark and icon should lean into this literally and avoid generic “tech platform” shapes (abstract blobs, speech bubbles, generic checkmarks) that say nothing about what the brand stands for.

Recommended direction

Compass needle / cardinal point as the icon — simplified to a single clean mark, not a literal illustrated compass face (avoid looking dated or like a travel-app icon).

The needle can double as a subtle “P” or arrow form to stay modern and abstract rather than literal-skeuomorphic.

Avoid rounded/organic blob shapes for the icon — they undercut the precision and direction the name promises.

Orange (#F39C12) works well as the needle tip / pointer color against a deep blue (#1B4F72) body — reinforcing “this points you somewhere specific,” not just “this is a nice rounded logo.”
